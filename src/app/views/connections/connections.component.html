{{ connectionTypeName }} - Process

What happens:

  • ✅ Stock: Filled Stock REDUCED (cylinder given to customer)
  • 💵 Income: Service Charge collected from customer
  • 💵 Income: Deposit Amount collected from customer

Steps:

  1. Select customer receiving the new connection
  2. Add product (cylinder type) they are receiving
  3. Enter Service Charge amount (if applicable)
  4. Enter Deposit amount collected from customer
  5. Save to record the new connection

What happens:

  • 🔄 Stock: Empty Stock INCREASED (cylinder returned by customer)
  • 💵 Income: Service Charge collected for transfer
  • 💸 Expense: Deposit Refund paid back to customer

Steps:

  1. Select customer who is transferring/returning connection
  2. Add product (cylinder type) being returned
  3. Enter Service Charge for processing transfer (can be 0)
  4. Enter Deposit Amount to refund to customer
  5. Save to record the transfer and refund deposit

What happens:

  • 🔚 Stock: Empty Stock INCREASED (cylinder returned by customer)
  • 💵 Income: Service Charge collected for surrender process
  • 💸 Expense: Deposit Refund paid back to customer

Steps:

  1. Select customer who is surrendering connection permanently
  2. Add product (cylinder type) being returned
  3. Enter Service Charge for processing surrender (can be 0)
  4. Enter Deposit Amount to refund to customer
  5. Save to record the surrender and refund deposit
